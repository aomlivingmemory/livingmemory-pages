Information you choose to save

LivingMemory can handle links and shared text, notes, photos, screenshots, videos, titles, and your reasons for saving an item. A saved item can also contain its source URL, available page title and description, preview image or site icon, date, status, and other organization details. Content you save may itself include personal information about you or others.

The app can generate text from images and video audio, and create derived categories, keywords, related-memory information, and a search index. These results depend on the content and available Apple system capabilities.

Where the app stores content

LivingMemory writes memory records to a SwiftData store in its app-private Application Support directory. Imported media, captured images, and downloaded link preview images are stored as files in that app-private directory. A derived search index is also stored there.

The Share Extension and Safari Web Extension stage captures and media in a shared App Group inbox on the device. The main app imports them into its private storage. A capture may remain pending if import has not completed, including when the free saving limit has been reached.

Connections to other services

For a saved web link, the app may request the linked webpage and image resources to obtain a title, description, preview image, or icon. The Share Extension may also ask Apple’s LinkPresentation framework for a preview while you are saving. For supported YouTube links, the app can request a thumbnail from i.ytimg.com. These requests contact the relevant website or image host; those services may receive normal network information associated with the request. A preview image is not a screenshot of the page.

Safari Smart Capture uses an explicit button press to capture the visible area of the active Safari tab and pass that image and URL to the app’s local shared inbox. LivingMemory does not send that captured image to a developer server.

The app uses Apple’s Photos picker, Vision, Speech, and Natural Language frameworks. The video transcription path may download Apple speech model assets if needed. This version of the app has no account sign-in, developer-operated sync service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party package dependency.

Subscriptions

LivingMemory Pro purchases and restores use Apple StoreKit. The app checks verified StoreKit entitlements to decide whether unlimited saving is available. LivingMemory does not send subscription transactions to a developer server or collect payment details itself.

Deleting saved content

You can delete an individual memory from the Library or its detail screen. The app deletes the record and attempts to remove media files used only by that memory; it reports if media removal fails. Its derived search index is updated from the remaining records. When the app identifies a duplicate shared capture, you can choose to discard the new capture.

Deleting a memory in LivingMemory does not remove copies you may have stored elsewhere. The app has no feature to manage device backups, no in-app delete-all control, and no developer account deletion flow.
